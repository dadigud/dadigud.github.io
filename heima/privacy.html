Heima: privacy policy

Heima is a private family day board used by a single household. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What Heima reads

With your permission, Heima reads one Google Calendar that you choose, read-only: the list of your calendars (so you can pick one) and the events in the chosen calendar. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

Where the data goes

The events are stored only on the household's own home server and shown on the family's tablet. Heima does not sell, share or send your data to anyone else, and it has no analytics or advertising.

Removing access

You can remove Heima's access at any time in your Google Account, under Security → Third-party apps & services. After that, the stored access token no longer works and Heima stops reading the calendar.

Contact

Questions about this app can be sent to the support address shown on Google's consent screen when you connect Heima.
