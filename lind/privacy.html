Lind: privacy policy

Lind is a private household app used by a single family. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What Lind reads

With your permission, Lind reads your Gmail read-only, and only the messages in your inbox from the school and kindergarten senders you list in Lind. It never sends, changes, labels or deletes anything in your Google account, and it does not mark messages as read. The rest of your mail is never fetched.

Where the data goes

Those school messages are read on the household's own home server. To pick out the dates in them, their text, their attachments and the newsletters they link to are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Lind keeps only each message's sender, subject and times and the dated items it found, not the message itself. Lind does not sell, share or send your data to anyone else, and it has no analytics or advertising.

Removing access

You can remove Lind's access at any time in your Google Account, under Security → Third-party apps & services. After that, the stored access token no longer works and Lind stops reading your mail.

Contact

Questions about this app can be sent to the support address shown on Google's consent screen when you connect Lind.
